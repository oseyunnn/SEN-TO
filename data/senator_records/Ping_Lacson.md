  Period: 2009-03-27
  Case_Number: DOJ I.S. No. XVI-INV-09C-00232

- [CLM-CON-002] On December 18, 2009, a DOJ panel issued a resolution finding probable cause for two counts of murder against Panfilo Lacson, leading to the filing of two murder informations in the Manila Regional Trial Court on January 7, 2010.
  Period: 2009-12-18–2010-01-07
  Case_Number: Criminal Cases Nos. 10272905 and 10272906
  Procedural_Status: FORMAL_CHARGE
  Senator's response: Lacson denied involvement.
  Evidence_Status: REPORTED
  Sources: [SRC-010]

- [CLM-CON-003] On February 4, 2010, the Manila Regional Trial Court found probable cause and ordered the issuance of an arrest warrant against Panfilo Lacson.
  Period: 2010-02-04
  Case_Number: Criminal Cases Nos. 10272905 and 10272906
  Procedural_Status: DECISION (interlocutory probable-cause order)
  Senator's response: Lacson sought reconsideration and petitioned the Court of Appeals for certiorari and prohibition.
  Evidence_Status: REPORTED
  Sources: [SRC-010]

- [CLM-CON-004] On February 3, 2011, the Court of Appeals nullified the trial court's probable-cause orders and dismissed the two murder informations against Panfilo Lacson for grave abuse of discretion, denying motions for reconsideration on March 18, 2011.
  Period: 2011-02-03–2011-03-18
  Case_Number: CA-G.R. SP No. 116057 / Criminal Cases Nos. 10272905 and 10272906
  Procedural_Status: DISMISSAL_OR_ACQUITTAL
  Evidence_Status: CORROBORATED
  Sources: [SRC-010, SRC-011]

- [CLM-CON-005] On June 8, 2011, the Supreme Court denied the Dacer family's petition challenging the Court of Appeals dismissal on procedural standing grounds (lack of conformity by the Office of the Solicitor General), expressly stating that it did not decide the substantive merits of the charges against Lacson.
  Period: 2011-06-08
  Case_Number: G.R. No. 196209
  Procedural_Status: DISMISSAL_OR_ACQUITTAL
  Context: Procedural ruling on private complainants' standing to appeal; not a factual adjudication of the underlying murder allegations.
  Evidence_Status: REPORTED
  Sources: [SRC-010]

- [CLM-CON-006] On July 22–23, 2026, Panfilo Lacson publicly alleged that his team had identified suspected ghost infrastructure projects and double appropriations in Taguig City—initially citing PHP 2.085 billion and subsequently raising the examined 2025 budget insertions to PHP 2.385 billion across slope-protection and drainage projects (mentioning entities linked to the Discaya family and Topnotch Catalyst Builders).
  Period: 2026-07-22–2026-07-23
  Procedural_Status: ALLEGATION
  Context: Allegations raised by Lacson against external infrastructure projects; not charges against Lacson and not independently audited losses or findings of guilt.
  Evidence_Status: CORROBORATED
  Sources: [SRC-012, SRC-013, SRC-014]
